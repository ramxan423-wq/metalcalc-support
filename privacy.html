MetalCalc Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy describes the MetalCalc iOS app and how information is handled.

Project information

MetalCalc provides metalworking calculators and planning tools. Measurements and project information entered into the app may be processed and stored locally on your device. Exporting or sharing a project sends it to the destination you select.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app subscriptions and payments. MetalCalc may receive subscription status and transaction identifiers needed to determine access to Pro features. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Support communications

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request.

Device permissions and third parties

Some features may request device permissions or invoke Apple sharing and file services. Those services are subject to their own policies. Any additional analytics, third-party SDKs, remote storage, or data collection in a released build must be reflected in an updated version of this policy and in App Store privacy disclosures.

Data retention and choices

You can remove locally stored app data using the app's available controls or by removing the app, subject to iOS backup behavior. For support email deletion requests, contact us below. Apple manages subscription and transaction records under its policies.

Children

MetalCalc is a technical productivity application and is not designed specifically for children.
