VisionBend Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

VisionBend Pro is a conduit bending calculation and camera-assisted measurement application. This policy describes the intended handling of information and should be checked against the actual released app and any third-party SDKs before publication.

Information and permissions

When you choose to use camera-based features, the app may request camera access to display or analyze what you point your device at. Calculations and measurements you enter may be processed to provide results. Access to photos or files, if offered, should occur only when you select those features.

Storage and sharing

Project settings and calculation history may be stored on your device. We do not intentionally require you to submit personal information to use basic calculations. We do not claim that no information is collected by platform services or any integrated third-party software; this must be verified for the release build.

Payments

If paid features are available, payments are processed by the relevant app store. The store's own privacy practices apply to purchase processing.

Retention and your choices

You can manage permissions in device settings. Locally stored app data may be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling, subject to your device's backup settings. For privacy requests, contact the address below.

Children

The app is designed as a technical utility, not specifically for children.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.
