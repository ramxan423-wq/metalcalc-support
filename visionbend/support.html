Common questions

Why is a calculated bend different from the physical result?

Check the measurement units, conduit dimensions, bend type, tool settings, and actual field conditions. Verify all measurements before cutting or bending.

Camera feature not working?

Check camera permission in your device settings, restart the app, and make sure there is sufficient light. Camera-based measurements may require calibration and independent verification.

How do I manage a subscription?

Use the subscription management section of your Apple App Store or Google Play account, as applicable.

Safety notice
