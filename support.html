MetalCalc Support

Help with MetalCalc metalworking calculators, CAD-related tools, and Pro subscriptions.

Contact

For assistance, bug reports, and feature requests, email ramxan423@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Subscriptions

MetalCalc Pro subscriptions are billed through Apple's App Store. To manage or cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

For purchase restoration, use the app's Restore Purchases option when available.
